The challenge

Risk hides between individual findings

Modern attacks rarely depend on a single critical vulnerability. They exploit combinations of weaknesses, assumptions, and control gaps.

Unknown exploit paths

A scanner may find isolated weaknesses but miss how an attacker can chain them into access to critical systems.

Security blind spots

Internal teams can overlook assumptions, inherited configurations, and attack techniques outside their daily view.

Unclear remediation priorities

Long findings lists make it difficult to decide which fixes reduce material business risk first.

Untested controls

Tools and policies may look complete on paper while failing under realistic adversary behavior.
