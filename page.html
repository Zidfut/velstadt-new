Privacy Policy

This page explains how Velstadt collects and uses information through this website.

July 2026 Last updated

1. Information collected

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate website usage and performance. It does not use advertising pixels or non-essential cookies.

If you send a message through the contact form, Specia1ne collects the information you provide:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Project / context
  • Message content

When you submit the contact form, the website also processes technical information including your IP address, source page, browser locale, timezone and submission time.

This information is used for message delivery, spam prevention, rate limiting, security and roubleshooting.

Hosting and security providers may also process basic technical information such as IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, referring source and time of access.

This is used for website delivery, privacy-friendly analytics, security, troubleshooting and basic operation.

2. How information is used

Information is used to:

  • Respond to your message
  • Understand project context
  • Understand aggregate website usage and performance
  • Maintain and protect the website
  • Prevent spam, abuse or technical issues
  • Comply with legal obligations when required

Comply with legal obligations when required.

3. Contact form

The contact form is handled through a server-side contact form system.

Messages are processed server-side and delivered by email so Specia1ne can respond to your inquiry or continue a relevant project conversation.

The contact form may use Cloudflare Turnstile to prevent spam and abuse. This may process basic technical signals required for verification.

Please do not send sensitive personal information through the form.

4. Cookies and analytics

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate website usage and performance.

The service reports visits, page views, referring sources, approximate country, device type, browser, operating system, page-load performance and Core Web Vitals.

Cloudflare states that Web Analytics does not use cookies or localStorage, does not fingerprint individual visitors, and does not collect or use visitors’ personal data for analytics.

The website does not use advertising pixels or non-essential analytics cookies. If additional analytics, cookies or similar technologies are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated and consent will be requested where required.

5. Project inquiries

Sending a message through the contact form does not create a client relationship.

Any project work, scope, timeline, fees, rights and responsibilities will be agreed separately in writing.
